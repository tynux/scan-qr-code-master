Privacy Policy — Scan QR Code Master

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Scan QR Code Master” (扫码大师)

English简体中文繁體中文

English

Your scans never leave your iPhone. Scan QR Code Master has no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK and no server of its own. Camera frames, decoding, generated codes, your history and the Safe Browsing check are all processed on the device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Scanned and created codes — one JSON file inside the app's own sandbox (Application Support/ScanQRCodeMaster/history.json). Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Preferences — language, Auto Scan, Auto Zoom, sound, vibration and the free-plan counters, in the app's UserDefaults.
  • Nothing is written to iCloud, and there is no sync or backup service operated by us.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhyWhen
CameraTo read QR codes and barcodesWhen you open the Scan tab
Photo Library (read)To decode a code from a picture you pickOnly when you tap “Photo”
Photo Library (add)To save a code you createdOnly when you tap “Save to Photos”
Contacts (write)To save the contact details inside a vCard QR codeOnly when you tap “Add to Contacts”
App Tracking TransparencyTo ask whether the free plan's ads may be personalised. You can decline and keep using every feature.On first launch of a build that shows ads

Camera frames are analysed in memory and are never recorded, saved or transmitted.

Advertising (free plan only)

The free plan shows a banner advertisement served by Google AdMob. To serve it, Google may collect device and usage information (such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app interaction data) as described in Google's partner sites policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Ad requests never include the codes you scanned, your history or your photos.
  • Where required (EEA/UK), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for consent before any personalised ad is requested, and you can change that choice later in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy options.
  • If you decline tracking, ads are requested in non-personalised mode (npa=1).
  • Buying Premium removes advertising completely.
  • In airplane mode the app works normally; only the banner cannot load.

What we never do

  • No account, e-mail address or phone number is required or collected.
  • No scan content, history, image or location is uploaded anywhere.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK of our own is embedded.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

  • Delete a single entry in History with a swipe, or clear everything in Settings → Clear history.
  • Export your history as CSV at any time, then delete it.
  • Revoke camera, photos or tracking access in iOS Settings at any time; scanning needs the camera, everything else keeps working.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data with it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Questions: peill@me.com.
